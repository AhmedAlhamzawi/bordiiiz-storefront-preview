# Bordiiiz — storefront preview

Design and speed reference for Bordiiiz (Boddies Board Games). Task BOR-5.

**This is not the shop.** No orders, no payment, no real prices. The real shop
will be a bought platform (see the `stack-decision` document on BOR-5) — our
difference is the games and the people, not our checkout code.

What this preview is for:

- Something the Founder can open on a phone today.
- The speed reference: 3.1 KB gzipped, zero external requests, one round trip.
- The field list Supply must fill per SKU: players, playtime, age, language,
  real stock count, delivery price, delivery days, and one line on why this game.

Pages: `index.html` (shop) and `product-codenames.html` (product).
